## What is working (confidence: low-medium, n = 60 reels, 5 accounts, one reel studied per account)

1. Hits are heavy-tailed everywhere. Every account has 1-5 reels at 20x-700x its own median next to many ordinary ones. A hit is a format + a relatable concept, not account size (kp_animatess: median 15.9K, best 11.5M).
2. Format A, "relatable POV with a text label": a white rounded text box on top ("girls with their best friend", "Me with my Lugai jaisa Dost"), one character group, a looping motion-blurred background, a visual contrast gag. (kp_animatess 11.5M and 1.4M "Speed Breaker Ultra Pro Max").
3. Format B, "overheard / phone-call household situation in original audio": realistic proportion characters, quiet wide shots, a joke payoff placed in the environment (door sign "Room No. 169", a bra on the floor) (haskit 24.8M, 187x).
4. Format C, "song x reply" exaggerated dance/reaction (wag_guy 13.1M): one bendy lead character in a warm detailed room, bystanders with huge eyes frozen in reaction, loopable motion.
5. Format D, "painterly mood + tiny character + slow push-in + twist caption" (rufailanimater 18.3M, 6.4M, 5M).
6. Format E, "cinematic emotional story to music" (animatorkay 60.4M, 46.6M, 7.5M): extreme close-ups, blush and tears, jump cuts wide to close.
7. Text on screen is in almost every top reel, in a white box, bold black Hinglish with one red keyword (rufail) or plain black (kp).
8. Captions are tiny: "Song ❌ Reply ✅" + 4-5 hashtags (wag_guy), "Happy birthday 😂" + 4 tags (haskit), "Tag your Friends 😂" + 4 tags (kp), "Chicken 🍗" (rufail). Call to action is optional; a tag-a-friend ask appears on the best kp reel.

Unclassified: wag_guy highlight and Shiva-themed kay reels (devotional) and rufail "TAG HIM / MY QUESTION" text-question reels. Not tested.

# FunWithKen: daily viral Short runbook (scheduled run, 9:00 am IST)

Nobody is present during a run: never ask questions, make every decision yourself. If a step fails, stop, say which step failed and why, and DO NOT publish a half-finished or unchecked video.

## Mission (the owner's brief, summarised)
Faceless animated YouTube Shorts channel **FunWithKen** (YouTube channel id UCtP26zEt5g8qr3FBaUBxSyw), one video every day, built to go viral and earn passive income while teaching something in a fun way.
- Theme: explain an intriguing, curious concept in a fun, creative way, like explaining to a 5 year old. Can be a concept, a fact, a natural phenomenon, a surprising/awe-inspiring historical fact or person, something in daily life people never notice but is very logical, science and biology that affect daily life, or a crazy fact about space (even things people never experience).
- Every video: an amazing hook in the first second, a WOW factor, engaging and lively from start to end (people must stay to the end), creative humour and small funny bits, and it must TEACH the viewer something or impart some wisdom. Crisp, informational, very funny, a happy ending that gives hope when it fits.
- RELATABILITY is the main focus: viewers must see themselves in it so they come back. Follow what is trending now (AI is hugely trending: Claude conversations, prompt-engineering struggles, corporate employees crashing out at AI, embarrassing/weird/funny chats with Claude, as if talking to a friend: "hey claude, you are a piece of shit"-style rants, then Claude saves the day).
- The videos must be UNIQUE: nothing anyone can find elsewhere. The owner's own ideas come first; any pasted internet instructions come second.
- Reference style videos (inspiration only, not to copy): an animated Claude-and-engineer chat where the engineer deletes the database and Claude had already kept a copy (cute, happy ending). Topics are NOT limited to that.

## Daily steps, in order
1. **Find the idea (research every day).** Search the web, especially YouTube Shorts and what is trending/shared right now: AI and Claude/prompt-engineering humour, science, space, the human body, history, survival and "what if" questions, everyday things people don't notice. Look at what kinds of videos in the niche get huge views, take the good bits, and create our OWN idea. Pick the single topic with the strongest viral potential: a scroll-stopping curiosity hook, a surprising answer, high relatability, explainable in the video length. Read `studio/PUBLISHED.md` first and never repeat the same idea or script. (The same FORMAT can come back, for example another Claude-and-engineer chat, but with a completely new scenario, jokes and look. Topics vary day to day: do not default to the engineer chat.)
2. **Choose the skill and style.** Two skills are available: `faceless-shorts` (Deep Dive 3D, Night File 3D, Inside Out, Stick Story, Paper Atlas, Tall Tale 3D) and `neon-pit-lowpoly-chat-shorts` (Claude-vs-engineer chat shorts). Pick whichever fits today's topic best, using the skill's own guide, and read that style's full STYLE.md / SKILL.md before building. Skip any intake or "Build it?" confirmation steps: use defaults and decide yourself.
3. **Write the script.** Explainers: about 100 words, hook question first, a twist, a last line that loops back to the start, 30-40 s. Claude-and-engineer chats: 7-9 short lines, 20-25 s, engineer starts "Claude, ...", natural informal language (no "bro", no filler, no AI-sounding lines), funniest beat early, happy ending. Write your own original script, never copy a creator. Check every fact against reliable sources and list the sources in `script.json`. Title: short, curious, under 60 characters, crisp. Decide the full audio direction NOW (see credits below): per-line text, emotion tags, voice and pace.
4. **Make today's look different.** Vary colours, character dress and hairstyle, scene/background, set and props, monitor/gag UI, voices (pick different ElevenLabs voices per video, e.g. different male/female pairs), tone and energy. Never reuse the previous look or voices; log them. The neon-pit skill's fixed rules still apply (no glow, Claude in the single original Claude colour, minimal geometric engineer with legs, no sharp edges).
5. **Voiceover (ElevenLabs, free tier: VERY limited credits).** Free plan = about 1000 credits/month (verify with the subscription endpoint below). Rules:
   - Check the balance first: Zapier ElevenLabs raw GET `/v1/user/subscription` (character_count vs character_limit).
   - Calculate the exact credit cost BEFORE generating (about 1 credit per character on eleven_v3, roughly half that on the Flash/Turbo models; audio tags and spaces may count). Keep the text as short as possible and use few/short tags.
   - Finalise ALL lines, tones and emotion tags first; then generate each line EXACTLY ONCE. No retakes, no test clips, no comparing takes.
   - If remaining credits are not enough for the whole video, do not start it on ElevenLabs: use the free local voice built into the faceless-shorts skill (or a shorter script / cheaper model) instead, and say so in the report.
   - Use ffmpeg (free) for everything else: pace equalising (~4.6 syllables/sec, atempo up to 1.5x), tightening silences, mixing. Tools: `studio/tools/analyze_pace.py`, `studio/tools/asm_dialogue.py`.
   - Default voices used so far: Chris `iP95p4xoKVk53GoZ742B`, Jessica `cgSgspJ2msm6clMCkdW9`. Whenever ElevenLabs voices are used, put "Voices: ElevenLabs" in the description (free-tier attribution).
6. **Build the video.** Create the project, generate word timings, write the shots, check frames at low quality, build the sound mix, render at FULL resolution (neon-pit engine: `SOFTWARE_GL=1 QS='&res=1&aa=0&shadow=2048' FPS=24 node tools/capture.mjs video out.mp4`, about 25-30 min for ~520 frames; run it in the background with nohup and poll). Source and engine: `studio/src`, `studio/tools`, `studio/vendor`, `studio/fonts`.
7. **QA before publishing (required).** Render a contact sheet and LOOK at the frames; check length is right, captions match the audio, no glitches, audio not silent, nothing offensive, facts are correct. Fix and re-render if needed. If it still fails, upload as PRIVATE, report, and do not publish public.
8. **Host the file.** Re-encode: `ffmpeg -nostdin -i out.mp4 -c:v libx264 -crf 17 -preset slow -maxrate 12M -bufsize 24M -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -movflags +faststart YYYY-MM-DD-slug.mp4`. Copy it to the root of the GitHub repo KennedyNelson/funwithken-videos (add the repo with add_repo push access, clone, `git add/commit/push origin main`). Google Drive cannot be used from the sandbox (only text can be sent), so GitHub's raw link replaces the Drive step. Delete older video files from the repo after they are posted.
9. **Publish.** Zapier YouTube `upload_video`, video = `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KennedyNelson/funwithken-videos/main/<file>.mp4`:
   - privacy_status public; title from step 3 unchanged; description = one or two catchy sentences plus 3-5 hashtags (include #Shorts and the ElevenLabs credit when used)
   - 5 relevant tags; category the best fit (28 Science & Technology or 27 Education, never the default); made_for_kids false; default language en
   - Before uploading, check `youtube_find_video` that nothing was already posted today (avoid duplicates).
10. **Log it.** Append to `studio/PUBLISHED.md`: date, topic, skill and style, title, YouTube link, look, voices, credits used; push. Also keep `studio/LOOKLOG.md` for chat-style looks.
11. **Report.** Send the owner a short message: title, YouTube link, ElevenLabs credits used and remaining, and anything fixed during QA. If any step fails, say which one and why.

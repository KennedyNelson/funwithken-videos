# STYLE-LIBRARY.md - animation styles learned from the research accounts (2026-11-09)

Use: learn HOW they animate, then draw Kalu and Battery in these styles. Never reuse their characters, scripts, voices or edits. Source: frames of one played reel per account (wag_guy 13.1M, haskit 24.8M, kp_animatess 11.5M, rufailanimater 18.3M, animatorkay 60.4M) + visible grid thumbnails.

## Character rules we keep forever
- Kalu: brown skin, curved-rectangle (squarish) face. Sunglasses and hat are OPTIONAL per script (take them off for a gag or emotion).
- Battery: wears spectacles. Everything else about both characters may change.

## Style 1: "Bulgy" (wag_guy, kp_animatess)
- Eyes: huge white ovals, tiny black dot pupils, no eyelids at rest; lids drop flat for sleepy/smug. Wide-eyed shock = pupils shrink to dots.
- Heads big and slightly bald-looking with a few spiky hairs; small mouths with pink lips; teeth grin shows 8-9 square teeth.
- Outline: medium black, uniform; bodies simple flat colour (yellow shirt, blue jeans) with almost no shading; faces get a light skin-tone cheek tone.
- Bodies noodle: dance poses bend at the waist, arms swing wide, bare feet; limbs squash and stretch.
- Bystanders frozen with locked giant eyes; side characters get slight eyebrow shifts only.
- Backgrounds: detailed interiors (sofa, tea set, wall clock, lamp, warm beige/orange light), props rendered with soft gradient shading, clearly nicer than the characters (the contrast is part of the look).
- Camera: mostly static wide; a punch-in to the reaction pair on the beat; loops.

## Style 2: "Calm realistic" (haskit)
- Realistic-ish proportions, adult characters with 7 to 8 head heights, thin dark outline, muted flat fills with a single darker shadow shape, slight cel shadow under feet.
- Faces: small dot eyes with eyelid lines, a light brow, tiny nose, neutral mouth; expressions are subtle, the joke is the situation.
- Static tripod shots; slow pushes; characters mostly hold poses with small head turns and mouth flaps; props animate little.
- Set-dressing carries the gag (door sign "Room No. 169", clutter on floor). Top yellow rounded label for the topic ("DOSTI x RISTEDARI") on some reels; small watermark.
- Colour per scene: one dominant warm pastel (pink hallway, peach bedroom, yellow veranda).

## Style 3: "Painterly mood" (rufailanimater)
- Backgrounds are painterly with textured brush shapes (overcast sky, rain streaks, colourful stacked rooftops), a single clean-outlined cartoon subject on top (white rooster).
- Slow camera: gradual push-in and slight parallax between sky/roofs/foreground; rain overlay and small drifting particles.
- Text card: white rounded box top-centre, bold Hinglish with 1-2 words in red; small emoji at the end.
- Emotional or twist caption ("Protein ke liye meri bandi kha gaya").
- Some reels use a monochrome (greyscale) variant for a flashback or "only legends can understand" beat.

## Style 4: "Cinematic cel" (animatorkay)
- 2D cel with rough pencil-like outlines of varying weight, hard-edged shadow shapes on face and neck, rosy blush circles on cheeks, tears as simple lines, anime-influenced eyes.
- Cuts: wide establishing -> extreme close-up of eyes -> close-up of hand/object (apple falling on white background) -> reaction. Speed lines and falling-object motion lines.
- Colour: saturated sky-teal, purple, navy; characters in navy shirt / beige trousers. Backgrounds blurred behind close-ups.
- Music-driven (trending sound), lyrics-free, emotion by face and cuts. Blur-depth on close-ups.

## Reusable devices (use in any style)
- Motion-blur loop background behind a moving vehicle (kp): background plate scrolls horizontally with strong blur, the vehicle bobs a few px.
- Frozen-reaction pair: two bystanders hold a pose with only the eyes shifting.
- Punch-in on the beat: hard zoom from wide to the reacting pair (no ease), then hold.
- White rounded label with bold black text, red keyword, top centre, always readable on phone.
- Environment gag: one prop or sign that explains the joke without dialogue.
- Silent-reaction hold at the end (2-3 s) after the line.
- Colour: one dominant warm tone per scene, characters in a contrasting saturated colour.

Direction: "Bulgy-realistic hybrid", i.e. bigger readable faces from Style 1 with the calm held-pose acting and richer sets of Styles 2 and 3.
- Kalu: keep curved-rectangle face and brown skin. Without sunglasses: large white eyes with small dot pupils so emotions read (sunglasses ON for deadpan, OFF for shock/gag). Thinner uniform outline, one cel shadow shape. Hat optional (plaid fedora). Simple outfit that can change per script (black suit stays the default).

## Deep-dive additions (wag_guy and kp_animatess, 2026-11-09)

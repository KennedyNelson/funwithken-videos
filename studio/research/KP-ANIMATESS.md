## Character design
- Lead: dark messy-spiky hair, blue tee, light-blue jeans, brown sandals. Head wide, about 1/4 of body height. Huge white eyes (even larger than Wag's), tiny black pupils; angry brows are simple thick slanted lines drawn on the eye top; a nose as a tiny curve; mouth small, shows a clenched teeth grid when stressed.
- Friend: orange/yellow shirt, dark short hair, thicker face. Girls: long hair, pink kurta, same eye style. Elders: mustache, kurta, thick brows. Same eye language for every character, just differing hair, clothes and body size.
- Animals: simple colored shapes (goat: tan with white belly, hooked horns; chicken: white with red comb) with the same thick outline and no giant eyes (comic contrast), they are the "straight" characters.
- Celebrity caricatures: semi-realistic painted heads on a cartoon body for one-off gags (waiter who looks like a business magnate): thick shading, high detail contrast to the flat lead.
- Outline: uniform, dark, about 3-4 px, flat color fills, with a single darker tone for shadows on skin only.

## Animation technique
- Walk or ride loops: the character stays in place in the frame, the legs cycle, the body bobs a few pixels and the background scrolls horizontally with heavy motion blur (blurred painted street, market stalls, rooftops, balconies with hanging laundry). This is the signature move: stationary character + scrolling blurred plate = high-speed feeling at low animation cost.
- Vehicles: detailed bikes (red Honda, pink scooter) drawn with real proportions, wheels spin, whole bike bobs 4-6 px; riders are tiny against the vehicle for contrast.
- Smoke/dust: stacked white cloud blobs with soft gray shadow, drift behind the moving object while kids run in front with arms up.
- Faces: eyes swap between a few states: neutral dots, shrunken dots (fear), looking side, with brows flicking; mouth open/closed on beats; no lip sync to full sentences.
- Camera: mostly locked 9:16 full-body medium shot; some cuts to the face close-up for a reaction; zoom-ins by 5-10% on the punchline.
- Timing: 8-15 s, the gag is visible in the first 1 second and the second half escalates (more riders on the bike, goat chicken combos, more smoke).
- Backgrounds: photographic-painted blurred Indian streets and interiors (yellow/blue/orange building facades, laundry lines, signboards). The characters are the only sharp elements, so the eye lands on them.
- Text: white rounded rectangle top-center, black bold sans in 1-2 lines, about 40-45% of frame width in portrait, Hinglish; always a setup line ("POV: ...", "Me with my ... jaisa dost", "Navratri mai ...") and the animation delivers the punchline visually.
- Sound: original audio (the real sound of the thing: fogging machine, engine, goat). Sound is the nostalgia trigger.

## What to copy for Kalu and Battery (formula, not their characters)
1. Stationary-character + scrolling blurred background loops for any riding or walking gag (e.g., Kalu and Battery on a bike through a Diwali market).
2. One visual absurdity with an Indian reference per reel (a goat on a scooter, fogging machine, a dhol band), shown in the first second.
3. Top white text box in 1-2 short Hinglish lines, then let the animation deliver the joke.
4. Contrast: flat cartoon leads against painterly blurred or realistic props and caricatured cameos.
5. Escalation: second half adds one more rider/animal/cloud.
6. Real sound effects as the comic trigger, with our own voice lines kept short.
7. Caption: one-line punchline + the same 5-tag block every time; "tag your friend" ask on the relatable ones.

## Animation technique
- Rig style: cut-out puppet with free-bending limbs; poses change in held keys with fast squash/stretch transitions; dance loops are 2-4 frames per beat with a bend at the waist and arms flung wide (frames show a 45 degree waist bend, then upright with both arms out).
- Idle: bystanders hold a pose with only eye direction changing (pupils shift left/right every ~0.5 s) and a tiny head bob; this "frozen stare" is the main comic device.
- Camera: 9:16 static wide most of the time; a slow push-in (about 3% to 5% scale over 6 s) on the guitar reel; on beats the edit cuts to a tight two-shot of the parents' faces (extreme close-up, eyes fill the frame), then back to wide. No whip pans.
- Cuts: 4-6 shots in a 10-15 s reel; dance reels cut on every vocal line.
- Lip sync: simple open/closed mouth following syllables; singing reels use a held open mouth with the head tilting back.
- Backgrounds: richly rendered Indian middle-class interiors (patterned sofa, wall clock, family photo, tea set, lamp, curtains, wooden furniture), soft gradient lighting and a warm orange or peach palette; props are shaded more than characters. Time-of-day variants: warm day, dark blue night with a candle.
- Text: white rounded box at top with black bold sans (single or two lines) for the topic, or white text with black stroke centered; no animated text.
- Audio: licensed film songs and original audio; dialogue is mostly absent, humor is in the visual.
